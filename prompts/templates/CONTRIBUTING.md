# Contributing to SK Hub

First off, thank you for considering contributing to SK Hub! Our goal is to build the most comprehensive open-source library for creative advertising and digital marketing.

## How Can You Contribute?
* **Marketing Templates:** Share your highly converted ad copy structures or campaign blueprints.
* **AI Prompts:** Submit tested prompts for Midjourney, Runway, or other AI generation models.
* **Design Assets:** Contribute vector layouts, structural formatting guides, or UI/UX workflows.

## Submission Guidelines
1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a new branch for your contribution (`git checkout -b feature/your-feature-name`).
3. Ensure your files are neatly formatted in Markdown (`.md`) or standard design formats.
4. Open a Pull Request with a detailed explanation of what your template or asset achieves.

Let's build the future of digital ecosystems together!
