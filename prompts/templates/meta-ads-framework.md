# Meta Ads structural Framework

A blueprint for developing conversion-oriented campaigns that align with current Meta Algorithm Dynamics.

## The 3-Phase Creative Structure
To combat ad fatigue and maximize ROI, all video and static creatives should follow this framework:

1. **The Hook (0-3 Seconds):**
   - *Goal:* Disrupt scrolling behavior.
   - *Method:* Question-based engagement hooks, high-contrast visuals, or dynamic motion tailored to specific consumer segments.

2. **The Core Value Proposition (3-10 Seconds):**
   - *Goal:* Establish authority and solve the pain point.
   - *Method:* Clean, minimalist design aesthetics featuring clear messaging, highlighting specific benefits rather than just features.

3. **Strategic Call to Action (CTA):**
   - *Goal:* Drive measurable results and specific actions.
   - *Method:* Direct instructions (e.g., "Tap to Claim Your Slot", "Download the Guide") with clear destination mapping.

## Technical Configuration Checklist
- Ensure precise multi-panel structural formatting for any carousel assets.
- Verify Meta Ad Set destination URLs to prevent mismatch errors.
- Implement structured budget splitting across distinct consumer segments.
