# AI Prompt Engineering Guidelines

This document outlines the strict structural formulas used to generate high-quality commercial assets, product mockups, and video content using AI models.

## 1. Video Generation Prompts
When generating video content, strictly adhere to the following 5-part structural formula. **Never mix this structure with image generation prompts.**

**Formula:** `[Scene] + [Element] + [Movement] + [Audio] + [Other]`

1. **Scene:** Location, time of day, atmosphere/mood, and background environment.
2. **Element:** Main subject, demographics, clothing, and foreground props/objects.
3. **Movement:** Camera motion (e.g., pan, tracking), Subject motion (e.g., walking, gesturing), and Environmental motion (e.g., wind, traffic).
4. **Audio:** Dialogue, ambient soundscapes, Foley, and music genre/mood.
5. **Other:** Visual style, resolution (e.g., 8K), aspect ratio (e.g., 9:16), Cinematic color grading, and lighting setup.

*Example:* 
`[Modern creative studio, golden hour, warm atmosphere] + [Young male digital marketer, casual professional attire, sitting at a desk with dual monitors] + [Slow push-in camera tracking, subject typing and looking up to smile] + [Lo-Fi chill ambient beat, subtle keyboard typing sounds] + [Cinematic color grading, 4K, 16:9, soft studio lighting]`

## 2. Image Generation & Visual Concepts
For generating commercial advertisement poster backgrounds, minimalism, and product mockups:

**Formula:** `[Core Subject] + [Environment/Background] + [Lighting & Aesthetic] + [Camera Details]`

*Example:* 
`[Minimalist premium skincare product bottle] + [Placed on a smooth stone podium, negative space around] + [Cinematic studio lighting, sharp object rendering, clean aesthetic] + [Macro photography, 85mm lens, high depth of field]`
